# Privacy Policy

**SomaFM miniplayer** does not collect, store, or send any personal data to
the developer. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no
user accounts.

The app does connect to other services on your behalf, and those services
necessarily see those connections. They are listed below.

## Network connections

The app connects to the internet only to do what you ask of it:

- **SomaFM** — to fetch the station list and to stream the station you select.
  As with any internet request, SomaFM receives your IP address and the usual
  connection details, and handles them under their own privacy policy:
  https://somafm.com/privacy/
- **Google, YouTube, Apple Music, or Spotify** — only when you explicitly
  choose to look up the currently playing track. That opens a search URL in
  your default browser, so the chosen service receives the track name and
  whatever your browser sends, under that service's privacy policy. The app
  itself sends nothing to these services.

## Data stored on your Mac

Your settings (last played and recent stations, volume, sorting, notification
and click preferences) and a cached copy of the station list are stored
locally in the app's container and never leave your device.

Because nothing is sent to the developer, there is no personal data held on
any server, and no data deletion request is applicable.
